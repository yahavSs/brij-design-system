[ 06 - What to expect ]
Common questions.
Do we need an AI team already?
No. We work equally well with teams who have existing ML capacity and with teams starting from zero. Our work fills the gap that exists today.
How is Brij different from an agency?
Agencies sell hours. Vendors sell licenses. We embed as a lab - our incentive is the advantage you compound, not the work we bill.
What about data and IP?
Everything we develop is yours. No gatekeeping, no lock-in. You own the models, the code, the training data, and the deployment pipeline.
How fast can we start?
A Compass engagement can begin within 10 days of signing. A Bridge build typically starts within three weeks once scope is agreed.
